## Brand & Style

This design system channels the kinetic, jubilant atmosphere of milestone celebrations. It balances energetic party ambiance with refined modern product craftsmanship. Built on a hybrid design movement combining **vibrant modernism** and **luminous glassmorphism**, the aesthetic captures the sensation of floating confetti, glittering champagne, and ambient party lighting while retaining razor-sharp usability.

The target audience spans event organizers, digital celebrants, and party guests who seek celebratory digital interactions that feel as memorable as physical gatherings. Every interface element aims to evoke feelings of anticipation, warmth, delight, and personal connection. Frosted glass planes float over warm, glowing atmospheric backdrops, punctuated by crisp celebratory typography, sparkling micro-details, and expressive interactive wish components.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an immersive, deep violet night canvas (`#120B1E`) that allows jewel-toned celebratory lights to glow with intense vibrancy. 

- **Primary (`#E6007A` - Radiant Magenta):** Powers key conversion points, celebratory actions, and joyful highlights. Conveys intense energy and modern festivity.
- **Secondary (`#FFB800` - Festive Gold):** Provides radiant warmth, celebratory badges, starburst motifs, and VIP celebration highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#8A2BE2` - Electric Purple):** Establishes nocturnal party depth, ambient gradients, and dimensional glow accents.
- **Neutral (`#120B1E` - Midnight Velvet):** The deep purple-black structural foundation that guarantees high contrast against sparkling overlays.
- **Accents:** Paired with an energetic **Warm Coral** (`#FF6B5B`) for interactive micro-states and lively reaction elements.

Background surfaces rely on tiered dark glass tiers rather than flat greys, allowing underlying magenta and gold ambient flares to diffuse subtly through interface panels.

## Typography

The type system blends the energetic, dynamic geometry of **Bricolage Grotesque** with the inviting, pristine readability of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headline Hierarchy:** Bricolage Grotesque provides expressive visual personality with its playful optical quirks, crisp curves, and rhythmic boldness. It leads hero moments, birthday countdown counters, and milestone banners.
- **Body & Label Hierarchy:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides friendly yet clear legibility across wish wall entries, message threads, guest listings, and form inputs.
- **Playful Accents:** Large festive headings may integrate celebratory decorative punctuation or custom script-styled emoji sparkles to heighten celebration cues without degrading text readability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a flexible 12-column fluid grid system on desktop, collapsing to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width container of 1280px with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates rich multi-column wish grids, interactive side panels, and floating confetti sidebars.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Adapts to balanced 2-column or 3-column card configurations with `1.5rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacks with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters for thumb-friendly reach.

Vertical cadence relies on the 4px base increment embedded in the spacing tokens, maintaining airy spatial breathing room between animated party sections while binding related wish cards and profile badges closely together.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is crafted through translucent glassmorphism backed by ambient chromatic illumination. Elements do not rely on harsh grey shadows; instead, they hover as luminous frosted surfaces over dark festive depths.

- **Base Canvas:** Deep violet night tone (`#120B1E`) layered with subtle radial gradients of diffused magenta and golden ember particles.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Feed Surfaces):** Semi-opaque tinted surface (`rgba(35, 18, 56, 0.65)`) with a `16px` backdrop-filter blur and a crisp `1px` translucent border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- **Level 2 (Interactive Wish Cards & Modals):** Frosted glass (`rgba(48, 24, 76, 0.75)`) with a `24px` backdrop blur, a top-edge highlight border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`), and a multi-layered tinted ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(18, 11, 30, 0.6)`, infused with a soft magenta glow (`0 0 24px rgba(230, 0, 122, 0.18)`).
- **Level 3 (Floating Action Bars & Popovers):** High-density frosted glass (`rgba(60, 28, 92, 0.88)`) with `32px` blur and an energetic rim lighting border (`1px solid rgba(255, 184, 0, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs **Rounded** geometry (scale level 2) to maintain a soft, friendly, and celebratory physical feel. Standard component corners use `0.5rem` (8px), expanding to `1rem` (16px) for cards, and `1.5rem` (24px) for prominent celebration cards, wish containers, and dialog surfaces. 

Interactive tags, action buttons, and reaction pills use fully pill-shaped contours (`9999px`) to invite touch and reinforce the buoyant, confetti-like personality of the interface.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Festive Action:** High-saturation gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #E6007A, #FF6B5B)`), crisp white text in bold label styling, pill-rounded geometry. On hover, it blooms with an ambient gold/magenta dual outer glow (`0 8px 24px rgba(230, 0, 122, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary / Glass Button:** Semi-transparent purple glass fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), white text, `1px` border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`), scaling slightly up on hover with brightened border illumination.
- **Tertiary Starburst Action:** Gold outline (`#FFB800`) with festive gold text and translucent fill on hover, used for celebration tips, confetti triggers, and gift actions.

### Cards & Wish Cards
- **Wish Card Surface:** Frosted glass backing with `rounded-xl` corners. Equipped with an interactive celebration reaction tray at the footer (sparkles, hearts, party poppers).
- **VIP Milestone Card:** Decorated with a subtle metallic golden top border rim, celebratory badge anchoring, and embedded gold foil shimmer gradient accents across headline text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Celebration Badges:** Compact pill-shaped status tokens with vibrant high-contrast backgrounds (e.g., Gold `#FFB800` fill with dark violet text for "Birthday Star", Electric Purple with white text for "Host").
- **Interactive Confetti Tags:** Micro-pills displaying celebration topics, hashtags, or attendee status with soft translucent backgrounds and bright primary color icons.

### Inputs & Text Areas
- **Greeting Message Inputs:** Translucent dark violet field (`rgba(25, 12, 40, 0.7)`), inset soft glow, `1px` subtle outline (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), shifting upon focus to a vibrant magenta border with an ambient `rgba(230, 0, 122, 0.25)` outer field ring.
- **Placeholder Styling:** Muted lavender text tone (`rgba(240, 230, 255, 0.5)`) pairing harmoniously with deep backgrounds.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Rounded checkboxes and circular radio controls filled with Electric Purple (`#8A2BE2`) in resting checked states, accompanied by a bright Festive Gold (`#FFB800`) checkmark or central pip.

### Festive Specialty Components
- **Floating Confetti Canvas:** An ambient interactive canvas component capable of emitting physics-based star particles and multi-hued confetti on celebratory milestones or wish submission.
- **Gift Countdown Counter:** Bold tabular numbers utilizing Bricolage Grotesque encased in frosted glass pods with gold metallic divider accents.
